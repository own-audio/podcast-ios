# An episode's page

Tap an episode in any list to open its page. It shows the picture, the show, the title, the
date and length (or the time left), and which source the episode comes from (**This iPhone** or
a server's name).

## Buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Play** / **Resume** | Plays from the start, or from where you stopped. Greyed out when it can't play now (offline and not downloaded). |
| **Played** / **Unplayed** | Marks the episode. |
| Download (arrow) | Downloads the episode. A ring shows the progress, a check that it is on the iPhone. Tap the check for **Remove Download**. After a failed download the button tries again. |
| **Play Next** | Puts it first in [Up Next](up-next.md). |
| **Play Later** | Puts it last in Up Next. |
| **In Up Next** | Shown once it is queued; tap it to take it out again. |

## Chapters

If the episode has chapters in its feed, they are listed under **Chapters** with their start
times. Tap one to play from there.

## Notes

The episode's show notes follow, with web addresses as links you can tap. You can select and
copy the text. **Episode Website** opens the episode's own page, when the feed gives one.

## Summary and questions (own.audio)

On an own.audio server that offers it, and only for an episode that has a transcript, the page
has a **Summary** section. Anywhere else it isn't there. See
[own.audio extras](own-audio.md#episode-summaries-and-questions).

## Another device was further along

When you play an episode from a server and another device got to a different place in it (more
than half a minute apart), the app asks **Continue from another device?**:

- **Continue from** the other device's time, or
- **Keep this iPhone's position**.

It never moves your position without asking.
