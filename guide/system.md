# Lock screen, widget and Siri

## Lock screen and Control Center

While an episode plays, the lock screen and Control Center show its title, show and picture,
with:

- play and pause,
- skip back and skip forward, by the seconds set in [Settings](settings.md#playback),
- the position bar, which you can drag,
- next and previous chapter, when the episode has chapters.

Headphone and car controls send the same commands.

## The widget

Add the **Up Next** widget from the Home Screen's widget gallery (touch and hold the Home
Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**, and find own.podcast). It comes in a small and a
medium size.

It shows the episode you are in, then episodes you have started, then the newest in New
Episodes; the small size shows one, the medium up to three, each with its show, picture and
time left. Tap an episode to play it. With nothing to show, it says **Nothing new yet**.

## Siri and Shortcuts

Two actions work with Siri without any setup, and are in the Shortcuts app:

- **Continue Listening**: goes on with the episode you were listening to. Say, for example,
  "Continue listening in own.podcast" or "Resume my podcast in own.podcast".
- **Play a Show**: plays a show you follow, the episode you are in or else its newest. Say
  "Play *show name* in own.podcast".

## AirPlay

Use the **AirPlay** button in the [player](player.md), or the one in Control Center, to play on
a speaker, a TV or other headphones.

## In the background

iOS lets the app check feeds and servers for new episodes in the background, about every hour
at most, so New Episodes is current when you open it and a show's download rules can fetch the
newest episodes, often overnight on Wi-Fi. iOS decides when this happens. If Background App
Refresh is off for the app in the iPhone's Settings, new episodes are found when you open it.
