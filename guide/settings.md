# Settings

Open Settings with the gear at the top of [New Episodes](new-episodes.md). **Done** closes it.

## Servers

Your servers, each with its kind and user name, and a dot for whether it answers. Tap one for
its [server page](servers.md#the-server-page); swipe one to the left to remove it.
**Add a Server** adds another. Shows you follow on this device need no server.

## Downloads and Offline Mode

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Downloads** | | Opens the list of downloads; shows how much space they take. |
| **Offline Mode** | off | Shows and plays only what is on this device. See [Offline Mode](downloads.md#offline-mode). |
| **Download on Wi-Fi Only** | on | Downloads, yours and the shows' own, wait for Wi-Fi. Not shown on a Mac. |
| **Keep at Most** | **No Limit** | A limit for all downloads: 1, 2, 5 or 10 GB. Over it, played episodes go first, then the ones not played for longest; never the one playing. |

A show can also keep its own downloads; see [Show Settings](shows.md#show-settings).

## Playback

| Setting | Choices | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Skip Back** | 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60, 90 s | 15 s | The skip back button, in the app and on the lock screen. |
| **Skip Forward** | 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60, 90 s | 30 s | The skip forward button. |
| **Speed** | 0.75× to 2× | 1× | The speed episodes start at. A show can have its own. |
| **Smart Rewind** | on / off | on | After a pause, goes back a few seconds (3 s after a short pause, up to 30 s after an hour). |
| **Play New Episodes After Up Next** | on / off | on | When Up Next runs out, the newest new episode plays on. |

## Streaming

| Setting | Choices | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **On Mobile Data** | **Stream**, **Downloaded Only** | **Stream** | **Downloaded Only** plays nothing that isn't on this device while on mobile data. Not shown on a Mac. |
| **Keep Ahead** | 5, 10, 15 min | 10 min | While an episode streams, this much of what comes next is kept on this device, on mobile data too. With Low Data Mode, 5 minutes. |

## Accent Colour

The colour of buttons, links and what's playing: **App Colour** (the default blue), Blue,
Indigo, Purple, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Teal, Brown or Graphite. The app icon and the
widget keep the app's colour.

## About

- **Version**: the app's version.
- **Source Code and Licence**: the code on GitHub (MPL-2.0).
- **Privacy**: the privacy policy.
- **Logs**: what the app wrote to its log since it started: failed requests, playback trouble,
  sync. **Share Log** saves it as a text file to attach to a bug report. Nothing is sent
  anywhere unless you share it, and addresses are logged without sign-in tokens.

## Language

The app is in English and Czech, following your iPhone's language. To change it for this app
only, use the iPhone's **Settings → Apps → own.podcast → Language**.
