A podcast player for iPhone and iPad that needs no account and no server: find a show, or paste its feed address, and listen.

Made for listening
• Speed with the voice's pitch kept, and a speed of its own for any show
• Chapters, where the show has them
• Skip back and forward by the seconds you choose, and smart rewind after a pause
• Sleep timer
• Lock screen and Control Center controls

What's new, first
• New Episodes opens on the unplayed episodes across your shows, newest first
• Each show's page with its episodes; each episode's notes with working links
• Up Next: episodes from any show, in the order you choose
• Search for a show by name, browse by category, or add one by its feed address

Offline
• Download single episodes, or let a show download its newest ones by itself
• Delete episodes once played, and keep at most a few per show
• Downloads go on in the background; Wi-Fi only and a storage limit if you want

Your place, kept
• Your position in every episode is saved on this iPhone first, so a lost signal never loses your place

With your own server
If you run one, add it, and your shows and positions follow you between devices:
• own.audio: your family's podcast library
• Audiobookshelf: your podcast libraries
• gPodder sync services, such as gpodder.net and podfetch
Several servers can be added at once, and a switch on New Episodes chooses which one you see.

Private by design: no account with us, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app talks only to the feeds and servers you choose, to Apple's podcast directory when you search, and to Apple or Google if you sign in with them.

In English and Czech. Free and open source under the MPL-2.0. Made by own.audio.
