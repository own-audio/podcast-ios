# Privacy policy — own.audio Podcasts

own.audio Podcasts is a podcast player. It fetches the feeds you add, and syncs with the
servers you choose: own.audio, Audiobookshelf, or a gPodder-API server. This page says what the
app keeps and what it sends. Last updated 17 October 2026.

## What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account with us, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no
server of ours that the app reports to. We, the makers of the app, receive no data from it.

## What the app sends, and to whom

- **To the publishers of the shows you follow**: the app fetches their feeds, artwork and
  episodes from wherever the feed says, as every podcast app does. Those hosts see your IP
  address and what they would see from any player.
- **To Apple's iTunes Search**, when you search for a show: the words you search for. To the
  Podcast Index instead, only if you add your own key for it.
- **To the servers you add**, and only to them: your sign-in, the shows you follow, the episodes
  you play, where you are in each, and what you have played. What a server does with them is up
  to whoever runs it — you, a friend, or a hosting service with its own policy (for own.audio's
  hosted service: https://www.own.audio/privacy/).
- **To Google or Apple**, only if you choose to sign in to an own.audio server with them: the
  sign-in happens on their page, and the app passes the result to your server.

## What stays on your iPhone

- The shows you follow, their episodes, your positions, your queue, downloads and artwork.
- The servers you added and how to sign in to them, in the iPhone's Keychain; a sign-in token,
  not your password, where the server gives one.
- Deleting the app removes all of it.

## Children

The app has no age-specific features and collects nothing from anyone.
